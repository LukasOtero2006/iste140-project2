Glossary

These are the key terms used throughout this guide, listed in alphabetical order. Many of them come up on the Designed to Hook You page, while others are the tools and settings tested in the 7-day experiment.

App Permissions
The settings that decide what an app is allowed to access or do on your phone, such as sending notifications, using the camera or knowing your location.
Attention Economy
An economic model in which human attention is treated as a scarce resource that companies compete to capture, often because more attention means more advertising revenue.
Autoplay
A feature that starts the next video or episode automatically when the current one ends.
Background App Refresh
A setting that lets an app update its content while you are not using it, so that fresh posts or messages are ready as soon as you open it.
Digital Wellbeing
A healthy and balanced relationship with technology, in which our devices support our life instead of taking it over.
Doomscrolling
We call this to the action of scrolling or watching a lot of short videos or pictures, most commonly used in Tiktok or Instagram Reels, because when you watch a video you have to phisically scroll down with your finger to watch the next video. It is called "doom" or "dumb2 because it kills your neuronas and makes you dumb and makes you loose your time.
Engagement Metrics
Numbers that companies use to measure how much people interact with their product, for example daily active users, time spent in the app, likes and clicks.
Focus Mode
A phone setting that silences notifications and hides distracting apps during a chosen activity, such as studying or sleeping. You decide in advance which people and apps are still allowed to reach you.
FOMO
Short for "fear of missing out", the anxious feeling that other people are having rewarding experiences without us.
Grayscale Mode
A display setting that turns the whole screen black and white. Without bright colors, apps become less visually appealing, which can make it easier to put the phone down.
Infinite Scroll
A design in which new content keeps loading as you move down the page, so the feed never reaches a natural end. This removes the small pauses that could remind us to stop.
Notification Service (APNs/FCM)
The platform service that delivers push notifications from an app's server to a phone.
Persuasive Design
Layout, timing and interaction choices that are meant to influence what users do, usually by making it easy to continue and hard to stop. At its most manipulative it is known as a dark pattern.
Pull-to-Refresh
A gesture in which you drag a feed downwards with your thumb to load new content. Some critics compare it to pulling the lever of a slot machine, because you never know what the next refresh will bring.
Push Notification
A short message that an app sends to your phone even when you are not using it, often shown as a banner, a sound or a red badge on the icon. Each one is an external cue that invites you to open the app.
Screen Time
The amount of time spent looking at a screen, and also the name of the iPhone feature that reports daily use, pickups and notifications and lets you set limits. I used it to record the data for my experiment.
Streak
A streak is a counter of how long you have been doing something on a app, normally a game or a social medi a app, Snapchat,for instance, uses a fire symbol with a number counting how many days you have chatted with a specific person.
Variable Reward
A reward or prize in some app that can come at any time, is not a daily or weekly reward, it simply is not programmed to come an specific time
